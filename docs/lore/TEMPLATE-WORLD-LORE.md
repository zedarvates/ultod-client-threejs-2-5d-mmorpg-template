# The Last Open Threshold

Status: `approved template canon`  
Version: `1.0`  
Language: English  
Visual direction: stylized low-poly 3D with hand-painted textures

## Purpose and Adaptation Rule

This document defines an original, standalone micro-setting for the public
Three.js template. It is distinctive enough to demonstrate coherent quests,
characters and artwork, but compact enough to rename or replace.

Template users may change names, factions, locations and future realms while
preserving the underlying gameplay contracts. No StoryCore code, names, story
content or visual identity is copied; only general structured-world patterns
such as locations, character arcs, artifacts and narrative acts informed the
organization of this document.

## High Concept

Many small realms were once connected by ancient portals called **Thresholds**.
After a catastrophe known as **the Severance**, almost every passage became
silent or unstable. The bright kingdom of **Lumeris** survives around the last
known portal that still opens: the **Glass Threshold**.

The player, known canonically as **the Wayfarer**, emerges from that portal with
no memory and no identifiable realm of origin. They can approach unstable
Thresholds without harm, making them both valuable and dangerous.

When **Princess Seris Vale**, scholar and living Anchor of the Glass Threshold,
is captured by her former mentor **Warden Orin Veyr**, the Wayfarer becomes the
only traveler capable of following the disturbance and preventing Lumeris from
being isolated forever.

## The Thresholds

Thresholds are ancient structures that connect real places in separate realms.
They do not create worlds and cannot choose imaginary destinations.

### Waylight rules

1. A Threshold connects two existing locations.
2. Every safe opening requires an **Anchor**, either a living person or a
   prepared artifact capable of stabilizing the passage.
3. Forcing a damaged Threshold may exchange terrain, creatures or memories
   between realms.
4. Waylight cannot create matter, resurrect the dead or erase consequences.
5. Anchors gradually absorb echoes from connected realms.
6. Each major crossing awakens a Wayfarer memory that may be genuine, borrowed
   or distorted.

These limitations make portal travel useful without turning it into unlimited
teleportation or consequence-free magic.

## Historical Eras

### The First Joining

The earliest Guardians learned to stabilize natural fractures between realms.

### The Age of Open Roads

Knowledge, travelers and goods crossed freely. The organization later called
the Lantern Guild began as a network of inter-realm caravans.

### The Severance

Several Thresholds were opened simultaneously during an unknown emergency.
Connections crossed, fragments of realms merged and at least one world vanished
from the network. Orin survived and witnessed the loss directly.

### The Silent Years

The surviving Guardians sealed almost every portal. Lumeris retained the Glass
Threshold under strict observation.

### The Last Opening

The Wayfarer emerges from the Glass Threshold, proving that at least one unknown
realm remains connected.

## Playable Realm: Lumeris

Only Lumeris is defined in the initial canon. Other realms remain unnamed so
template users can extend the network freely.

### Hearthmere

A welcoming market village and the player's starting area. It contains Borin's
shop, an inn, forge, notice board and the first optional quests.

### Dawnwatch Keep

The royal residence overlooking Hearthmere. King Aldric governs here while
Seris's Conservatory studies the Threshold chambers beneath the keep.

### The Glass Threshold

A circular portal surrounded by translucent ancient stone. Its surface reflects
places that do not currently exist in Lumeris.

### Whisperwood

A bright forest affected by portal leakage. Foreign plants, displaced creatures
and small fragments of unknown terrain periodically appear among its trees.

### The Broken Watchtower

A ruined border tower occupied during the prologue by Orin's followers. Seris
is held here after attempting to stop the first sealing operation.

### The Silent Road

An abandoned trade route ending at dormant portal arches. It is the canonical
extension point for future realms.

## Factions

### The Crown of Lumeris

Protects the population and supports cautious restoration of the Glass
Threshold. Its central tension is safety versus responsibility toward isolated
realms.

### The Lantern Guild

Merchants, cartographers and explorers descended from portal caravan crews.
They provide trade, appraisal, delivery and exploration quests. Some members
value discovery; others primarily value profit.

### The Threshold Conservatory

Scholars led by Princess Seris. They preserve maps, portal fragments and
historical records. Their experiments are valuable but not always safe.

### The Closed Circle

Followers of Warden Orin who believe all Thresholds must be permanently sealed.
They are antagonists, not monsters: many have seen portal instability destroy
homes or families.

## Main Characters

### The Wayfarer

The customizable player character. Their name, appearance and equipment are
not fixed, but three facts are canonical:

- they emerged from an unknown Threshold;
- their memories are fragmented;
- unstable portals react to their presence.

Their long arc moves from an outsider searching for an origin to a traveler who
must decide which realms should remain connected.

### Princess Seris Vale

Royal heir, Threshold scholar and living Anchor. Seris deliberately confronted
Orin before her capture. After the prologue she becomes an active field ally and
organizes a new network of trusted explorers.

Arc: carrying the burden alone to sharing knowledge and responsibility.

### Warden Orin Veyr

Former chief Guardian and Seris's previous mentor. Orin believes isolation is
the only reliable protection against another Severance. He knows something
about the Wayfarer's origin but will not reveal it until the player understands
the cost of opening a realm.

Arc: protector transformed into absolutist antagonist, with a possible path
toward reconciliation rather than mandatory death.

### King Aldric Vale

A protective father and pragmatic ruler. He supports the Conservatory while
fearing that Seris will be consumed by the same research that caused the
Severance.

### Merchant Borin

A senior Lantern Guild member and former portal caravan guide. His shop
introduces trade, equipment, ownership and item appraisal. His apparent
opportunism conceals extensive knowledge of the old roads.

### The Captive Guardian

The introductory creature was displaced and restrained through forced portal
travel. Defeating it breaks the restraint rather than defining all foreign
creatures as evil.

## Character Relationships

| Relationship | Starting state | Long-term tension |
|---|---|---|
| Seris ↔ Orin | Former student and mentor | Shared knowledge, opposite conclusions |
| Seris ↔ Aldric | Loving daughter and father | Duty to other realms versus protection of Lumeris |
| Wayfarer ↔ Orin | Mirrored survivors | Uncertain origin versus certainty born from trauma |
| Wayfarer ↔ Borin | Cautious transaction | Trust earned through trade and exploration |
| Wayfarer ↔ Seris | Rescuer and rescued scholar | Partnership between traveler and Anchor |

## Canonical Artifacts

### The Wayglass Compass

Points toward viable realm connections rather than geographic north.

### Seris's Anchor Prism

Measures, stores and releases controlled Waylight resonance.

### The Warden Seal

Orin's broken badge of office. It can lock a Threshold from either side.

### The Lantern Ledger

Borin's enchanted trade book. It records ownership and debt when goods cross
between realms.

### The Shard of the Lost Realm

A fragment recovered after the Severance. It contains an impossible landscape
and may be connected to the Wayfarer's origin.

## Narrative Structure

### Prologue: The Last Open Threshold

1. The Wayfarer appears near Hearthmere.
2. King Aldric asks them to find Seris and grants a small equipment advance.
3. Borin supplies a starter weapon.
4. The player crosses Whisperwood.
5. The Captive Guardian is freed from its restraint.
6. Seris is rescued at the Broken Watchtower.
7. Seris reveals that Orin intends to seal the Glass Threshold permanently.

This prologue teaches movement, dialogue, commerce, inventory, light combat and
quest progression.

### Act I: Echoes on the Silent Road

The Wayfarer, Seris and Borin investigate three abandoned portal stations. Each
reveals a memory fragment, a different consequence of the Severance and an
argument for or against reopening the network.

### Act II: The Warden's Choice

Orin temporarily opens a damaged Threshold to demonstrate the network's danger.
The player discovers that some isolated realms may not survive without renewed
connection.

### Act III: The Realm Beyond Glass

The Wayfarer chooses between permanent closure, controlled restoration or a new
pact between realms. No ending is mandatory for downstream template projects.

## Gameplay Loop

**Explore → discover an echo → help a character or faction → obtain or trade an
artifact → stabilize a Threshold → unlock a route.**

Quest families:

- main story;
- portal anomaly investigation;
- merchant and delivery contracts;
- faction reputation;
- creature rescue or containment;
- artifact recovery;
- personal memory fragments.

## Visual Canon

- Stylized low-poly 3D with hand-painted textures.
- Strong silhouettes readable from the fixed isometric camera.
- Expressive but non-chibi proportions.
- Warm gold, green and terracotta for Lumeris.
- Cyan and turquoise for stable Waylight.
- Violet, pale gray and fragmented black for unstable Thresholds.
- The Closed Circle uses muted versions of Lumeris colors to show a shared
  cultural origin.

Every critical character, artifact and interactable must remain recognizable at
tablet resolution without relying on text labels.

## Extension Rules

1. New realms must define a distinct rule, biome and reason for connection.
2. Portals never solve conflicts without a cost or limitation.
3. Factions should contain internally reasonable positions.
4. New artifacts require an origin, owner, gameplay purpose and narrative cost.
5. Memory fragments may be unreliable but must not invalidate player choices.
6. Official template additions remain original and non-canonical to Ultimate
   Odycer unless explicitly promoted in a separate process.

## Current Demo Migration

The published `v0.1.2` demo predates this canon. Future implementation work must
align it without changing the approved lore:

- rename `Princess Elara` to `Princess Seris Vale`;
- present the `Cave Beast` as the **Captive Guardian**;
- change the encounter outcome from killing the creature to breaking its
  restraint and releasing it;
- identify the rescue location as the **Broken Watchtower**;
- introduce the Glass Threshold and Wayfarer arrival in the opening sequence;
- preserve King Aldric and Merchant Borin, whose names already match the canon.

This section is a migration reminder, not proof that the current runtime already
implements the lore.
